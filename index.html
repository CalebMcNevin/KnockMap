"Census Homes" counts only lawncare-eligible occupied dwellings (houses; apartment and condo units are excluded), so it undercounts total residences in dense areas. Census figures lag today's housing stock, and ACS values are estimates, not counts. US Census ZCTAs approximate (but don't exactly match) postal zip codes, so some zips appear as circles.

How to get your report CSV

  1. In WEMMS, open the Door Knocking module.
  2. Go to Reports, then DK Summary By Zip/FSA.
  3. Choose whatever settings you like, then click Export to save the CSV file.
  4. Come back here and drop that CSV file on the page (or click to browse).
