Census figures lag today's housing stock, and ACS values are estimates, not counts. US Census ZCTAs approximate (but don't exactly match) postal zip codes, so some zips appear as circles. "CensusHomes" counts occupied private dwellings only; seasonal and vacant homes are excluded.

How to get your report CSV

  1. In WEMMS, open the Door Knocking module.
  2. Go to Reports, then DK Summary By Zip/FSA.
  3. Choose whatever settings you like, then click Export to save the CSV file.
  4. Come back here and drop that CSV file on the page (or click to browse).

Permit requirements

Records live in this browser only. Export to move them to another computer. A zip/FSA is not a legal jurisdiction: keep the jurisdiction field pointing at the city or county whose rule this is.
